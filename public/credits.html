Credits

Sidequest's character art comes from the open-source Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) community. Thank you to every artist who shares their work.

Wings

Butterfly, moth, pixie, and dragonfly wings by The Foreman, from LPC Fairy Wings Pack 2. Licensed under OGA-BY 4.0 or CC-BY-SA 4.0. Colors were adjusted and frames were arranged into a sprite sheet for Sidequest.

The author's dedication: "In dedication to my grandmother, Sharon Rowe."

Faces

Original base by Stephen Challener (Redshrike), expressions by ElizaWy, mapped to all frames by JaidynReiman. Licensed under OGA-BY 3.0. See the LPC head sprites and ULPC Expanded Expressions.

Character bodies, hair, clothing, and weapons

Made with the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator, using art by many LPC contributors.
